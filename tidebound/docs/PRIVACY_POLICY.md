[Studio name] ("we") makes Tidebound, a game on Roblox. This policy explains what we store when you play and what you can do about it.

## What we store

We store your game progress (for example your Drifters, Silver, quests and settings), a record of purchases you make in Tidebound, and the reports you make about other players. We link this to your Roblox user id only. We never ask for or store your name, age, email address, phone number or location. The full list is in our data inventory: [link].

## Why we store it

We use it to save your progress, give you what you bought, keep the game fair, stop cheating, and understand which parts of the game players enjoy. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## Children

Many of our players are children. Tidebound has no free-text chat; players talk only with preset phrases and emotes. We do not collect information that identifies a child. Roblox's own parental controls (chat, spending limits and purchase approval) apply in Tidebound, and the game adds its own monthly spending limit.

## Notifications

If you allow Tidebound notifications in Roblox, we may send up to two a day (for example when crops are ready). You can turn each type off in Settings or turn notifications off in Roblox.

## Who we share it with

Roblox runs the game and processes purchases under its own privacy policy. We use [hosting provider] to run a small service that sends notifications and counts game events. Nobody else receives your data.

## How long we keep it

Game progress stays until you delete it. Purchase records are kept for [7] years for tax and fraud reasons. Game event logs are deleted after 13 months.

## Your choices

- See your data: Settings > Your data > See my data.
- Delete your data: Settings > Your data > Delete my Tidebound data, or ask Roblox to delete your account data (we receive and act on Roblox's erasure requests).
- Questions: [contact email].

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell players in the game.
